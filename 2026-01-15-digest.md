2. **[NVIDIA publishes TTT‑E2E — a test‑time training method letting LLMs learn during inference with constant runtime](https://cc554gl0.r.us-east-2.awstrack.me/L0/https:%2F%2Fapp.alphasignal.ai%2Fc%3Fuid=aZGOCm7tHMvJ9f1l%26cid=dea607b73011c7ad%26lid=wpVKdcvTmpGyilN3/2/010f019bbd965679-e2e5c620-1199-4641-877f-3815e83595a9-000000/yqf2A98GrvNrvYhfFj1RaBTjWwA=246)**  
   Source: AlphaSignal (Top Paper)  
   Summary: **NVIDIA** introduces End‑to‑End Test‑Time Training (TTT‑E2E) to compress long context into parameters by updating weights during inference. The result: constant per-token latency at extreme context lengths and large speedups vs full-attention approaches, making long-context LLMs far more practical.  
   > 2.7× faster at 128K tokens and 35× faster at 2M tokens (reported on H100).

3. **[OpenCode — a fully open-source local coding agent that plugs into terminals, IDEs, and 75+ model providers](https://cc554gl0.r.us-east-2.awstrack.me/L0/https:%2F%2Fapp.alphasignal.ai%2Fc%3Fuid=aZGOCm7tHMvJ9f1l%26cid=dea607b73011c7ad%26lid=1eucw0HmxYG9LUv3S/2/010f019bbd965679-e2e5c620-1199-4641-877f-3815e83595a9-000000/voc7bMlNIezTZYXb%5Fv21a5nlMiA=246)**
